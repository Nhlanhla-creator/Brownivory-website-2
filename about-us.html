Our Story

Inspired by the Brown Ivory Tree

At BIG, our name and mission are inspired by the enduring symbolism of the Brown Ivory tree—a native to Africa's savannas and woodlands. Renowned for its robust, durable nature and its ability to flourish under challenging conditions, the Brown Ivory tree embodies strength, resilience, and versatility. These values drive everything we do at BIG as we partner with forward-thinking enterprises and high-growth organizations to help them thrive in any business climate.

Founded with a vision to empower ambitious organizations, BIG has evolved into a comprehensive ecosystem addressing diverse challenges and opportunities. From strategic consulting and capital access to back-office support and impactful community engagement, BIG is your partner in building a sustainable, successful future.
